Relevant current text:
A broker or dealer shall not effect or enter into a contract for the purchase or sale of a
security (other than an exempted security, government security, municipal security, commercial
paper, bankers' acceptances, or commercial bills) that provides for payment of funds and delivery
of securities later than the first business day after the date of the contract unless otherwise
expressly agreed to by the parties at the time of the transaction.

Application in L.C.:
U.S. equities use T+1 under this paragraph. U.S. Treasuries are government securities excluded
by the paragraph; their T+1 profile is explicitly labelled market convention instead of law.

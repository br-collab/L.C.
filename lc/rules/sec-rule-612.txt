Relevant rule and effective-date facts:
For an NMS stock priced at or above $1.00, the legacy minimum pricing increment is $0.01.
The 2024 amendment adds a $0.005 increment when the stock's time-weighted average quoted spread
for its evaluation period is $0.015 or less; otherwise the increment is $0.01.

Release 34-105656 granted temporary exemptive relief from compliance with amended Rule 612 until
the first business day of November 2027. Therefore L.C. applies the legacy $0.01 increment before
2027-11-01 and uses the declared STANDARD or NARROW_SPREAD bucket from that date. For prices below
$1.00 the rule's $0.0001 floor remains applicable.
